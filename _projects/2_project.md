My first PhD chapter evaluated how well the latest generation of climate models represents tropical South American summer precipitation.

This work is presented in my paper, {% cite badarunnisa2026cmip6 %}.

In addition to the paper's analysis, I employed Taylor diagrams, traffic-light plots, and emergent constraints to compare model performance, identify strengths and weaknesses, and assess whether model characteristics are linked to realistic climate behavior.
